# Brindle Vale — the starting region

**Tiles** x 350–1450, z 2900–3900. **Levels** 1–4. **Time** ~6.5 h (prologue 2.5 h, side quests 3.5 h, contract
0.5 h). **Mood**: a gentle green valley at the end of summer: birch woods, hay meadows, a slow river, a chapel ruin on
the hill, bees. Under it, the first cold of the failing Oath.

Main quests here: MQ01–MQ04 ([main-quest-1.md](../main-quest-1.md)).

### Roads
- **Pilgrim Road** (dirt, 1 tile): Pilgrim's Shrine (480, 3380) → (600, 3370) → Brindleford's square (900, 3350)
  (crossing the ford) → (1000, 3280) → Tallow Green (1200, 3050) → (1320, 3010) → Hob's Tower (1400, 3000) → leaves
  the region east toward the Old Toll Bridge.
- **South Road** (dirt): Brindleford (900, 3360) → (1000, 3420) → Hanging Oak (1000, 3480) → (1200, 3460) → east
  across the Middle Downs to Millbrook (blocked at the region's edge (1450, 3450) by a Regency barricade until MQ04).
- **Mill Lane**: Brindleford → north along the east bank → Brindle Mill (870, 3240).
- **Chapel Path**: from the Pilgrim Road at (1000, 3280) up Chapel Hill to the chapel (1080, 3180).
- **Shepherds' track** (faint): Brindleford → south-east → Mosshill → Mossjaw Cave (1300, 3650).

## Places

### Pilgrim's Shrine (480, 3380) — where the game starts
A roadside shrine: a carved stone post with a lantern niche, an offering bowl, a bench. The hero wakes beside it at
dusk. In the bowl: a rusty knife, 3 copper. Behind it, the pilgrim road vanishes west into the mountains (the map's
edge: a fallen rockslide closes it, "the pass is closed").

A small village of 8 houses round a triangular green with an old oak; beekeepers and candle-makers; the smell of wax.

### Chapel of the Quiet Bell (1080, 3180) and the Bellwarden's Tomb
- **The ruin**: a small stone chapel on the hilltop, roofless, its square bell tower standing to half height with the
  great bronze bell still hanging (green with age, no tongue). Broken walls, a fallen arch, an altar, rubble; graves
  outside on the slope.
- **The crypt** (MQ02): stairs down behind the altar, sealed by an oath-iron grate. Three halls:
  1. *The Ossuary*: skull niches, 2 skeletons, a hidden niche (Hamund's journal page, 15 copper).
  2. *The Bell Hall*: the old bell-ringers' tombs; 2 ghosts; a pit trap across the middle (a rope bridge over it).
  3. *The Warden's Rest*: Sir Hamund's tomb; the crypt lord fight. A side room: a chest (a **bronze shield**,
     30 copper).
- After MQ02 the crypt stays quiet (if Hamund rested) or holds 3 skeletons that come back every 3 days (if destroyed).

### Brindle Mill (870, 3240)
A watermill, wheel turning on the river, a grain loft, a stable. Dunstan grinds the vale's flour. SQ-BV1.

### Red Hen camp (620, 3560)
In a birch clearing in the Birchwood: a 5x5 palisade, two tents, a fire, a weapon rack, crates of stolen pilgrim
goods, the gate facing east (toward the road), torches. 5 bandits (level 2–3) and Brannoc (level 4). A **stock**
(wooden pillory) by the fire: Wat is locked in it (SQ-BV7). The chest: the hero's belongings (their coin: 12 copper; a
**traveller's pack**: +4 bag slots), stolen pilgrim goods (60 copper's worth). After MQ03 the camp stays empty.

### Mossjaw Cave (1300, 3650)
A cave mouth in a gorse-covered knoll on Mosshill's west side. Two chambers and a deep tunnel (SQ-BV6): wolves at the
mouth (they den there), bats in the first chamber, spiders in the second, the **brood mother** (giant spider, level 4)
in the deep tunnel; a side crack where Wenna hides with her dog.

### Hob's Tower (1400, 3000)
A ruined round watchtower on the North Rise's east end, overlooking the Pilgrim Road where it leaves the vale. At its
foot the Regency's checkpoint: a barricade of carts, a tent, **Sergeant Matthias Crow** and 3 soldiers. At night,
something howls in the tower (CT-BV1).

## Side quests

### SQ-BV1 — The Flour Thief
- **Giver**: Dunstan, at the mill (or the notice board: *"Flour stolen. Reward. — Dunstan"*). **Level** 2.
  **Time** 25 min. **Opens** after MQ01.
1. Sacks go missing from the loft at night. Dunstan suspects "that orphan".
2. Watch the mill at night (wait until midnight in the loft): **Kit** creeps in and sweeps spilled flour into a rag;
   he doesn't touch the sacks. Later, **Jory** loads two sacks onto a hand-cart.
3. Follow Jory to the **Hanging Oak**: he leaves the sacks for the Red Hen's men, takes coin from the hollow. (If MQ03
   is done and the camp's gone, he leaves them anyway, and nobody comes: he's left waiting, and the hero can confront
   him there.)
4. Confront Jory: he owes the Red Hen money from dice; they threatened the mill.
5. Choice (`bv_flour`):
   - **Tell Dunstan the truth** (`jory_told`): Dunstan beats his son and makes him work it off; Dunstan +, Jory sour.
     Brindleford +0.
   - **Make Jory stop and pay it back quietly** (`jory_quiet`): Jory's grateful; Dunstan never knows. Brindleford +1
     (Jory tells everyone the stranger is decent).
   - **Report Jory to Pell** (`jory_reeve`): Jory's put in the stocks a week. Regency +5, Brindleford -1.
   - In any case, **speak for Kit** (optional): Dunstan takes Kit on as mill boy (`kit_at_mill`): Kit gets a bed and
     a wage; affects SQ-BV2's ending and the epilogue.
- **Rewards**: 80 xp; 25 copper from Dunstan; a **flour sack** (food: 5 bread).

### SQ-BV2 — Kit's Father (spans regions)
- **Giver**: Kit, after SQ-BV1 or on the third talk. **Level** 2–9 (stages). **Time** 30 min in all.
1. Kit's father, **Pip Tanner**, went to Kingsmere four years ago to earn money and never came back. Kit has his
   father's whittled wooden heron. *"If you go to Kingsmere, will you ask?"*
2. **Kingsmere** (any time after MQ04): the barracks' roll says Pip Tanner was pressed into the Regency's levy and
   deserted three years ago: "hanged if found".
3. **Pip** is Wren's second (met in MQ06 at the tax cart, or at the Hollow Oak in MQ09). Show him the wooden heron:
   he sits down hard. He's ashamed: he couldn't go home a deserter.
4. Choice (`kit`):
   - **Bring Kit to his father** at the Hollow Oak (needs Greenhood ≥ 0): Kit grows up an outlaw (`kit_with_pip`).
   - **Bring Pip home** to Brindleford (needs Pip willing: Greenhood ≥ 20, or Regency pardon via SQ-HM4's ending):
     (`pip_home`): he becomes the Cobbes' farmhand. Brindleford +1, Greenhood -5 (Wren loses her second).
   - **Lie to Kit** that his father is dead, "for his good" (`kit_lied`): Kit stays at the mill; he finds out in the
     epilogue.
   - If Pip was **captured** in MQ10 and hanged in SQ-HM4, the hero brings Kit the news (`pip_hanged`).
- **Rewards**: 150 xp; Kit gives the hero his father's wooden heron back "to remember" (a trinket, +1 luck).

### SQ-BV3 — Honey and Wax
- **Giver**: Agna Bee, Tallow Green. **Level** 3. **Time** 30 min. **Opens** after MQ02.
1. Agna's bees are deserting their hives, swarming west and dying in the meadow. Little Brede says the bees "go to the
   cold stones and fall asleep".
2. **The Nine Sisters** (750, 3050): frost on the stones in daylight; the barrow mound in the middle has been dug into:
   a fresh hole, and an **oath-iron grave-ward** (a spiked iron disk) thrown aside in the grass. Footprints: a man's
   boots, candle-wax drips.
3. Night at the stones: 3 ghosts rise from the hole (level 3) and the **Barrow-Maiden** (ghost, level 4).
4. Set the grave-ward back over the grave (only the hero can carry it). The cold leaves the stones.
5. The wax leads to **Hal Wicke**: he dug for barrow gold to pay the bride-price Garrick demands for Elsa (see
   SQ-BV8). Choice (`hal_secret`):
   - **Tell Agna and Goody Thatch** (`told`): Hal is shamed; Tallow Green fines him; Tallow Green +1, Hal -.
   - **Keep his secret** (`kept`): Hal swears never again; he'll owe the hero (SQ-BV8 goes easier).
- **Rewards**: 120 xp; Agna's **honeycomb** ×5 (food, heals over time); the hives return (Tallow Green's honey stocked).

### SQ-BV4 — A Bell With No Tongue
- **Giver**: Father Cuthwin, on being given the Bell-Tongue (MQ02). **Level** 3. **Time** 25 min.
1. Cuthwin wants the bell to ring again, by hand, "so the living ring it, not the dead".
2. The bell's yoke is rotten: **Tobin** must forge a new iron yoke-pin (bring him 2 iron bars: from the Red Hen camp's
   weapon rack or bought in Tallow Green; or 30 copper).
3. Climb the half-ruined bell tower (a climbing route: ledges, a ladder to repair with a plank) and hang the tongue.
4. Ring it at dawn: all Brindleford comes up the hill. Cuthwin, moved, tells the hero why he left the Order: *"The
   Lector asked us to speak words over the dead in the undercroft. Not prayers. Orders. I wouldn't. I've never told
   anyone."* (`cuthwin_story`: evidence against Anselm for SQ-LM5 / MQ22.)
5. Cuthwin teaches the **Rite of Rest** (`rite_of_rest`): lets the hero lay certain dead to rest instead of
   destroying them (Edric in MQ14, others).
- **Rewards**: 120 xp; Brindleford +1 (`bv_bell_rung`); the Rite of Rest.

### SQ-BV5 — The Reeve's Ledger
- **Giver**: Reeve Pell, nervous, after MQ03. **Level** 3. **Time** 25 min.
1. A Regency auditor, **Clerk Ansel Pike**, is coming down the Pilgrim Road to inspect Brindleford's books. Pell
   wants the hero to "delay him a day" on the road.
2. Looking into it: Pell keeps **two ledgers**. The true one shows a better harvest than the one he sends to Kingsmere;
   he's kept back a third of the tax every year so the village eats through winter.
3. Choice (`pell_ledger`):
   - **Delay the auditor** (scare his horse off, or a game of dice at the inn that lasts the night) and say nothing
     (`hidden`): Brindleford +1, Regency -5. Pell owes the hero.
   - **Give the true ledger to the auditor** (`reported`): Pell is taken to Kingsmere; the new reeve, **Grimwald**,
     is hard; Brindleford -2, Regency +15. (Grimwald makes Brindleford's epilogue darker.)
   - **Fix it honestly** (needs MQ02 done): show Pike the chapel's dead and the damage; he writes the missing tax off
     as "losses to the risen dead" (`written_off`): Regency +0, Brindleford +1, Pell +.
- **Rewards**: 100 xp; 40 copper (from Pell) or 60 (from the auditor).

### SQ-BV6 — Mossjaw
- **Giver**: Ada Cobbe, frantic (or Wenna's dog Scrap, who runs to the hero, barking). **Level** 4. **Time** 35 min.
  **Opens** after MQ01.
1. Wenna went after a lost ewe two days ago and hasn't come back. Scrap leads the hero across the Shepherds' track.
2. **Mossjaw Cave**: 3 wolves (level 3) den at the mouth; in the first chamber, bats; the ewe's remains.
3. The second chamber: spider webs, 3 spiders (level 3). Wenna's crook.
4. The deep tunnel: the **brood mother** (giant spider, level 4). Behind her, in a side crack too narrow for her:
   Wenna, starving, alive.
5. Lead Wenna out (she's slow; spiderlings attack on the way back).
- **Rewards**: 160 xp; the Cobbes' **wool cloak** (+warmth, armour); Brindleford +1; Wenna gives a shepherd's
  **sling** (a ranged weapon, weak).

### SQ-BV7 — The Smith's Apprentice
- **Giver**: Tobin Harrow. **Level** 3. **Time** 20 min (or 10 if done during MQ03).
1. Wat ran off three days ago with Tobin's best blade "to be a bandit". Tobin wants him back, alive.
2. Wat is at the **Red Hen camp**, locked in the stock: he refused to rob an old woman pilgrim and Brannoc's men are
   "teaching him". (If MQ03 is done before this starts, the hero found Wat in the stock already and he's back at the
   smithy: this quest completes at once.)
3. Free him; bring him home.
4. Tobin, grateful, shows the hero his grandfather's **smith-book**: drawings of a crown, an anvil "of barrow iron", a
   blue fire. *"My people were forge-keepers at the Lantern's abbey, before the Order took the forge for its own. I
   could find my way round an old forge, if I had to."* (`tobin_at_forge` becomes possible for MQ23.)
- **Rewards**: 100 xp; Tobin's blade (the hero can keep it: **Harrow steel**, a good sword for level 4) or return it
  (Tobin forges the hero a fine piece later: a free upgrade at his smithy).

### SQ-BV8 — A Candle for Elsa
- **Giver**: Elsa, crying in the inn's yard; or Hal at Tallow Green. **Level** 4. **Time** 30 min. **Opens** after
  MQ03.
1. Elsa and Hal Wicke want to marry. Garrick refuses: a candle-maker's son has nothing, and "the Fenns don't marry
   beneath the water". (He's afraid: a husband means questions about the past, about money he can't explain.)
2. Talk to Hal (if SQ-BV3 is done and Hal's secret `kept`, he's honest about the barrow; if `told`, he's sullen).
3. Talk to Garrick: three tries at persuasion, each using something the hero has done:
   - the bell rung (`bv_bell_rung`): *"You gave this village its courage back."*
   - his secret (any of MQ11's): *"You don't want a son-in-law asking where the money came from."* (He goes white.)
   - Elsa's happiness, plainly (always available, weakest).
4. Success: Garrick relents; the **wedding** at the Ferryman's Rest (a short scene: music, dancing on the square, Kit
   steals cake, Tobin gives a speech of one word: *"Good."*) (`bv_elsa_wed`). Garrick, after, drinks with the hero
   and trusts them (`garrick_trusts`).
5. Failure: Elsa and Hal plan to run off to Kingsmere; the hero can help them (`bv_elsa_eloped`: Garrick is broken,
   Brindleford -1, Elsa runs the Kingsmere bakery in the epilogue).
- **Rewards**: 120 xp; Brindleford +1 (wedding); a wedding candle (a light that never burns down).

## Contract

### CT-BV1 — The Watch at Hob's Tower
- **Notice board** (Brindleford, after MQ02): *"Something howls in Hob's Tower at night. My lads won't stand watch.
  60 copper. — Sgt. M. Crow."* **Level** 4. **Time** 25 min.
1. Sergeant Crow: three nights of howling; a soldier went up and came down white-haired.
2. Night in the tower: two **skeleton hounds** (**new**: skeletal wolves, level 3) and the ghost of **Corporal Hob
   Ashdown** (ghost, level 5), a watchman from the Barrow War, still on watch, howling the alarm for an enemy three
   hundred years gone.
3. Investigate first (optional): **Pell's archive** in the reeve's house holds the tower's old garrison roll and the
   last order never delivered: *"The war is done. Stand down the watch."*
4. Choice (`hob_watch`):
   - **Give him the order** (`stood_down`): he salutes and fades; the hounds lie down and crumble. Lantern +5.
   - **Destroy him** (`destroyed`): a hard fight; he drops his **watchman's horn** (blow it: enemies nearby flee
     briefly).
- **Rewards**: 120 xp; 60 copper; Sergeant Crow's thanks (the checkpoint waves the hero through without the writ).
