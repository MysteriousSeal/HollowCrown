# Hollowmere — Kingsmere, the lake, Reedby, Elderwick (and the Middle Downs)

**Tiles** x 1450–2750, z 1400–2700 (the Middle Downs: 1450–2600, 2700–3800). **Levels** 4–8 (the lake's isle 9–11).
**Time** ~5 h of side content (8 side quests, 2 contracts). **Mood**: the heart of the Vale: a wide grey-blue lake, reed
beds, herons, a walled town of red roofs; under it, a kingdom holding its breath.

Main quests here: MQ05, MQ06, MQ11 (sometimes), MQ12, MQ16 (start), MQ20, MQ21, MQ24, MQ25.

## Reedby — village (green at 1550, 2050)
A fishing village on the reed shore: houses on stilts, drying racks, eel traps, the Reedby ferry (to the isle after
MQ11, to Kingsmere always). Standing 0. People: ferrywoman **Agnes Lark** (MQ12), headman **Tom Weir**, the widow
**Hester Cole** (begged for her grain in MQ06), fisher-boys **Dab** and **Finch** Weir (SQ-HM1).

## Elderwick — village (centre at 2600, 1600)
A village of elder hedges and orchards on the north-east shore, the last place before the barrow hills: a wooden
palisade, a watch-bell, nervous people. Standing 0. People: reeve **Margery Cotes** (sharp, practical), the bell-ringer
**Old Simeon**, the orchard-keeper **Dunn**. Attacked by the dead in MQ22 (`elderwick` saved or burned); SQ-HM7
prepares it.

## Drowned Chantry (2050, 1950) — the lake's isle
MQ12's dungeon; afterwards the isle is quiet, the ferry visits; the wreck of the royal ferry stays beached as a
landmark.

## Weed Rats' camp (1700, 2400)
A camp of reed huts and a stockade on a hummock in the reed beds, boats pulled up; 5 raiders (bandits, level 5) and
their leader **Lanky Moll** (bandit chief, level 6). SQ-HM1.

## Side quests

### SQ-HM1 — The Weed Rats
- **Giver**: Tom Weir, Reedby's headman. **Level** 6. **Time** 30 min.
1. Lake raiders take a "toll" of fish and coin from every Reedby boat; they took Hester Cole's last coin.
2. The **Weed Rats' camp** in the reeds (by boat or wading at night).
3. Twist: two of the raiders are Tom Weir's own sons, **Dab** and **Finch**, gone over because Reedby starves since
   the Regency's fish tax.
4. Choice (`hm_weed_rats`):
   - **Clear the camp** (fight; the sons fight too unless the hero stops at Lanky Moll) (`cleared`): Reedby +1 (+2 if
     the sons are spared and sent home).
   - **Deal**: Moll leaves Reedby alone if the hero brings her the tax clerk's route (`dealt`): Reedby +1, Regency -10.
   - **Recruit them for the Greenhood** (needs Greenhood ≥ 20) (`to_greenhood`): Greenhood +10; Reedby +1.
- **Rewards**: 200 xp; 60 silver (Tom) or Moll's boat (a rowing boat at Reedby: the hero can row the lake).

### SQ-HM2 — The Sunken Hall
- **Giver**: a book in the Lantern Chapel (Brother Leofric), or Agnes Lark's story. **Level** 7. **Time** 35 min.
1. Osric's first hall sank when the lake rose 300 years ago; its treasure was never found.
2. Find a way down: the hall's roof breaks the water at low lake (a dry summer: now); hold breath swimming (a breath
   bar) through three flooded rooms; drowned skeletons; an air pocket in the treasury.
3. In the treasury: Osric's **marriage stone**, carved with the Oath as a marriage pact between Osric and Sigrun (the
   first proof that the Oath was a wedding, not a conquest) (`osric_stone`: lore; adds a dialogue with Hrathgar and
   unlocks SQ-BR4's last step).
- **Rewards**: 220 xp; **Osric's ring** (+1 to all attributes); 150 silver of old coin.

### SQ-HM3 — Aldous's Lesson
- **Giver**: Aldous, in secret, after MQ05. **Level** 6. **Time** 40 min.
1. Aldous wants to see "the real Vale" without his father's guards. He sneaks out at night; the hero is to take him
   to Reedby and back by dawn.
2. Three stops, each a lesson the hero chooses how to frame:
   - **Reedby**: a family whose grain was taken in MQ06's cart; Aldous gives his cloak away.
   - **The Weed Rats' shore** (or the Kingsbrook bridge): Greenhood archers; Aldous is recognised; the hero talks them
     down or fights (Aldous sees how the hero handles it).
   - **The gallows** at dawn on the way back: a poacher about to hang.
3. Aldous asks the hero at each: "Was my father right?" The hero's answers are counted (`aldous_just` if two or more
   are for mercy/justice, `aldous_hard` if two or more are for order/strength).
4. Corvin finds out (he always does); he's furious or, if Regency ≥ 40, quietly grateful.
- **Rewards**: 180 xp; Aldous's **signet of the heir** (Regency guards treat the hero as Trusted); Regency +5 or -5.

### SQ-HM4 — The Gallows Choir
- **Giver**: **Molly Fletch**, begging at the gallows. **Level** 7. **Time** 35 min. **Opens** after MQ06.
1. Her brother **Tam** is to hang at dawn for poaching a royal swan (if MQ10 was `warned`, it's **Pip Tanner**, Kit's
   father, who's to hang; the quest is the same, with higher stakes).
2. Ways to save him:
   - **The law**: find the courthouse's old charter: royal swans are the *king's*, and there is no king; the charge
     is void. Argue it before the magistrate (needs Regency ≥ 0 or the heir's signet) (`hm_gallows: law`): he's freed;
     Regency -0, Kingsmere +1.
   - **The rope**: cut him free at the gallows at dawn with Greenhood help (needs Greenhood ≥ 20) (`rescue`):
     Greenhood +15, Regency -20, Kingsmere -1 (a riot).
   - **The purse**: buy a pardon from Benedikt Orme (100 silver) (`bribe`): Orme's corruption noted (SQ-HM5 clue).
   - **Let it be** (`hanged`): Molly curses the hero; Kingsmere -1; if it's Pip, `pip_hanged`.
- **Rewards**: 200 xp.

### SQ-HM5 — The Steward's Pen
- **Giver**: Corvin (if Regency ≥ 20) or Hiram Bose (if `mq06_cart: split`). **Level** 8. **Time** 40 min.
1. Someone in the Regent's Hall sells its secrets to Carrow: garrison numbers, the tax routes.
2. Clues: a copied ledger page at the Heron and Pike (Dannet sells it); a Carrow-blue thread on a document seal; Orme's
   bribe-taking (from SQ-HM4's `bribe`, if done); Sabeline's own words (from MQ15's `sabeline_played`).
3. The mole: **Benedikt Orme**, the steward. Twist: he sold secrets to pay the Hall guards' wages, because Corvin's
   treasury couldn't; Corvin didn't know.
4. Choice (`orme`):
   - **Expose him to Corvin** (`exposed`): Orme hanged; Regency +15; Sabeline loses her eyes in the Hall (Act II
     ambushes don't happen).
   - **Turn him** (feed Sabeline false news through him) (`turned`): Regency +10; Sabeline's MQ24 offer comes with
     her soldiers already compromised (the Carrow pact counts as 1 ally, not 2).
   - **Let him go** with a warning (`warned`): nothing changes; Orme owes the hero (he'll leave the water-gate unbarred
     in MQ20).
- **Rewards**: 220 xp; 120 silver (Corvin) or Orme's key ring.

### SQ-HM6 — Lights in the Reeds
- **Giver**: Elderwick's fishers, via Reeve Margery Cotes. **Level** 8. **Time** 30 min.
1. Lights over the lake at night lure boats onto the rocks off Elderwick: two fishers drowned.
2. Out on the water at night (Elderwick's boat): the lights are **will-o'-the-wisps**: ghosts of the courtiers who
   drowned trying to save the king (they jumped in after him), still searching.
3. Choice: the **Rite of Rest** (`rite_of_rest`) lays them to rest (`hm_lights: rested`); else, fight (4 ghosts,
   level 8) (`destroyed`). Laid to rest, one tells the hero: *"The princess... the ferryman pulled her out. We saw. We
   saw from the water."* (`saw_maelis_saved`: a hint, and a piece of evidence that helps MQ21.)
- **Rewards**: 180 xp; Elderwick +1.

### SQ-HM7 — Elderwick's Wall
- **Giver**: Reeve Margery Cotes. **Level** 8. **Time** 35 min. **Opens** after MQ12.
1. Elderwick is the closest village to the barrows; the dead come nearer each month. Margery wants the palisade
   finished before winter.
2. Three tasks (any order): timber from the north-shore woods (fell 6 trees' worth: carry logs, or pay a woodcutter
   crew from Oakhallow); iron for the gate from Gunnhild (40 silver, or SQ-BV7's Tobin forges it free); a bell from
   Kingsmere's bell-founder to replace the cracked watch-bell (fetch it by cart; a draugr ambush on the north shore).
3. Done: the palisade, the gate, the bell (`elderwick_wall`): in MQ22, Elderwick holds on its own even without the
   hero (`elderwick: saved`).
- **Rewards**: 200 xp; Elderwick +2.

### SQ-HM8 — The Nurse's Garden
- **Giver**: Old Nurse Bettony, in the palace garden (she talks to the roses). **Level** 6. **Time** 25 min.
1. She raised the princess; she believes Maelis lives because "her rosebush still flowers". She asks the hero to
   bring her the princess's old music box from the shut-up royal apartments in the Hall (sneak in, or ask Aldous).
2. The apartments: dust, the princess's room as it was; the music box; a letter from Edric to Maelis, unsent, the
   night before Still Water: *"Tomorrow I sign away the barrows. Forgive me. Corvin says the dead will wake. I say we
   will starve. One of us is wrong."* (`edric_letter`: evidence: Edric knew; Corvin's argument confirmed.)
3. Bring Bettony the music box: she plays it and weeps. In Act III, if Maelis is in Kingsmere, Bettony recognises her
   at once (a public witness: Maelis's claim to the crown is believed).
- **Rewards**: 150 xp; Kingsmere +1; the letter.

## Contracts

### CT-HM1 — The Toll-Keeper
- **Notice board** (Kingsmere): *"Travellers robbed and killed at the Old Toll Bridge. The Regency pays 120 silver."*
  **Level** 6. **Time** 20 min.
1. The bridge (1900, 3000): bones in the stream, coins on the bridge's parapet, untouched.
2. The **Toll-Keeper**: a draugr (level 7) in rusted royal livery who rises at dusk demanding "the king's penny";
   travellers who couldn't pay died.
3. Pay him a royal penny (an old coin: from SQ-HM2's treasure, or the Sunken Hall's floor) and he lets the hero pass
   and fades (`toll: paid`), or fight him (he calls 2 skeletons).
- **Rewards**: 160 xp; 120 silver; title *Bridgekeeper* if paid.

### CT-HM2 — The Weeping Willows
- **Notice board** (Reedby): *"Something sings in the Weeping Willows and folk walk into the lake. 80 silver.
  — T. Weir."* **Level** 7. **Time** 20 min.
1. At the willows (1800, 2350) at night, a song: a **ghost** (level 8) of a young woman, **Lissa**, who drowned herself
   there after her betrothed was hanged by the Regency for poaching, singing the drowned to her.
2. Her betrothed's grave (an unmarked one outside Kingsmere's walls): bring his ring to the willows and she goes
   quiet (`willows: rested`), or fight her and her two drowned (ghosts).
- **Rewards**: 140 xp; 80 silver; Reedby +1.
