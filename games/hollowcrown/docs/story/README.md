# Hollowcrown — Story Bible

The whole story of Hollowcrown: the world, its people, the main quest, every side quest and contract, the romances,
the choices and the endings. The game's content is built from this; when something is made in the game, it is made as
written here (or this is changed first).

**Rating: PEGI 18.** The bar is The Witcher 3's storytelling, with nothing borrowed. Read
[writing-guide.md](writing-guide.md) first: tone, themes and their rules, the hero's voice, how quests are built.

World: 4096 x 4096 tiles, 2 towns, 10 villages, 12 dungeons (8 crypts, 4 caves), 6 bandit camps and the Greenhood's
camp, the White Fields, 8 regions (the Middle Downs between them).

## Conventions
- **Coordinates**: tiles `(x, z)`, x east, z south; 4096 a side; the start is the Pilgrim's Shrine `(480, 3380)`.
- **Ids**: `MQ01`..`MQ29`; side quests `SQ-BV1` (BV Brindle Vale, HM Hollowmere, SR Saltreach, GW Greenwood, LM Lantern
  Moors, SF Southfields, BR the Barrows); contracts `CT-BV1` likewise.
- **Flags** in `code`: the world state, all in [choices.md](choices.md).
- **Reputation**: Regency, Greenhood, Lantern -100..100; places -3..+3; written `Regency +10`, `Brindleford +1`.
- **Dialogue tones**: Kind, Hard, Sly, Blunt ([writing-guide.md](writing-guide.md)).
- **Enemies** are the 23 types of [bestiary.md](bestiary.md): beasts (wolves, boars, bears, spiders), people (bandits,
  Regency herons, Greenhood hoods, Lantern knights, Carrow mercenaries), the Oath's dead (ghosts, skeletons, draugr,
  barrow-hounds, barrow-giants, crypt lords) and the Vale's horrors (the Hungry, the Drowned, the Ashen, Bloomers, the
  Hollowed, the Sewn, pit-eaters, carrion rooks).

## The deeper truths (spoilers for the writers)
- The Oath's founding story is a lie: Osric won by massacre (**the Night of Lanterns** at Cairnfold), took Sigrun by
  force, and the crown is a chain on Hrathgar. The Barrowborn are the burned people's descendants.
- **Grave-poppy** pays for every faction: the Lantern grows it, the Regency taxes it, the Greenhood sells it, Carrow buys
  it; its diggers wake more dead than the broken crown does.
- **The Night of Still Water**: Corvin let the king drown and left two witnesses to drown; Garrick rowed away from them
  and turned back for the princess.
- Every main character has a stain and a wound ([characters.md](characters.md)).
