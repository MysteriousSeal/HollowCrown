# The Barrows — the barrow hills, the White Fields, the Great Barrow

**Tiles** x 1450–3000, z 150–1400. **Levels** 12–18. **Time** ~4.5 h of side content (6 side quests, 2 contracts).
**Mood**: bare grey-green hills ringed with barrows, cairns on every ridge, ruined watchtowers, ravens, wind; on the
southern slopes, the White Fields: miles of poppy on the graves, white as snow in summer, novices in masks moving
through them; in the north, in Act III, under a black sky, the Great Barrow.

Main quests here: MQ26, MQ27, MQ28.

## Access
- **The Fringe** (z 800–1400): open from MQ12. Grave-robbers, cairns, towers, Sigrun's Cairn, the White Fields.
- **The Barrow Valley** (z 150–800): the Lantern's oath-iron gate at the Watchers' Crypt (2250, 1050) until MQ26.

## Side quests

### SQ-BR1 — The Grave-Robbers
- **Giver**: Margery Cotes (Elderwick's market sells barrow-iron), or Odalys. **Level** 12. **Time** 35 min.
1. Barrows opened at night; the dead in them wake angry.
2. **Haskel the Spade**: the robbers dig in oath-iron gloves that blacken their hands, and load barrow-iron for Carrow's
   buyers. Two of them have lost hands to it; Haskel pays them in milk for the pain.
3. Choice (`grave_robbers`): **clear the camp** (`cleared`: half an ally at the siege); **follow the carts** and catch
   Sabeline's agent (`agent_caught`: Sigrun's torc recovered; Regency +10); **take a cut** (`bribed`: 150 silver; MQ22's
   Elderwick attack worse).
- **Rewards**: 260 xp.

### SQ-BR2 — The Last Watchman
- **Giver**: a ghost at the first Broken Tower (1700, 1250). **Level** 13. **Time** 40 min.
1. The Watchers, the first Lantern knights, each hold a third of an oath Sir Eadric Long made and broke.
2. Three towers: stand a night's watch against the dead; answer a riddle in the Watchers' tradition; bring a token of
   living kin (Novice Bryn descends from one).
3. **The turn**: the Watchers' oath isn't a vow of vigil. It's an oath of *penance*: they were Osric's men at Cairnfold,
   and swore to guard the barrows as long as their sons' sons lived, for what they'd done. The Order forgot it.
   (`watchers_oath`: Sir Eadric fights for the hero in MQ26.)
- **Rewards**: 280 xp; the **Watcher's cloak**.

### SQ-BR3 — Iron in the Hills
- **Giver**: the hero finds the adit, or Wren's scouts. **Level** 13. **Time** 35 min.
1. Carrow prospectors under **Joss Valt** drive a mine into a barrow hill, with a "permit" signed by Orme in the Regent's
   name.
2. They've broken into a barrow chamber; its dead are waking; the miners are trapped; Valt wants to blast through anyway.
3. Choice (`adit`): **collapse it** after getting the miners out (`collapsed`); **collapse it with them in** (`sealed_in`);
   **let them dig** (`dug`: 200 silver; a wave more at the siege; Carrow's shadow on the epilogue).
- **Rewards**: 240 xp.

### SQ-BR4 — The Oath-Bride
- **Giver**: the white roses at Sigrun's Cairn: touching them, a woman's voice in the old tongue. **Level** 14.
  **Time** 50 min.
1. Robbers broke Sigrun's cairn last winter and took her **bridal torc**; her ghost, bound to the broken cairn, fades.
2. The torc: from Haskel's goods to Sabeline's agent (`agent_caught`: already in hand), or to a Kingsmere goldsmith.
3. Rebuild the cairn (nine white stones), return the torc.
4. **Sigrun** speaks. Not a love story: *"They took me from the hall while my mother burned. That night Osric made me his
   wife, on the floor, with my mother's smoke in the doorway. In the morning my father knelt so I would live, and they
   called it peace, and they made songs."* (Implied; the camera stays on her face and the roses.) She asks the hero for
   nothing but to remember it, and to say it where the songs are sung. With `osric_stone` she confirms the scratched line
   was hers.
5. (`sigrun_freed`): she fights for the living at the siege, beside the oath-dead, *for her children's children* (the
   Barrowborn are hers and Osric's too: the kings' line and the grey-eyes are one blood, which no king ever admitted);
   walks with the hero in MQ28; makes laying Hrathgar to rest possible.
- **Rewards**: 320 xp; Sigrun's **white rose**.

### SQ-BR5 — Cairns of the Fallen
- **Giver**: the Hermit (SQ-LM4), or Grey Edda (SQ-HM10). **Level** 12. **Time** 30 min (spread).
1. Nine war cairns across the fringe; at each, a name stone in barrow-tongue, and an encounter.
2. **The turn**: the names aren't warriors'. They're the Cairnfold dead, children's names among them, carried out of the
   ash by the survivors and buried in the hills where the lowlanders wouldn't find them.
3. Bring the names to Edda, the Hermit or Sigrun (`cairn_names`): the barrow-tongue greeting; Hrathgar answers it in the
   dreams and MQ28 (`hrathgar_rapport` +1).
- **Rewards**: 200 xp; the **cairn-stone ring**.

### SQ-BR6 — The White Fields
- **Giver**: a novice girl, **Ide** (15), who runs out of the poppy to the hero on the Barrow Road. **Level** 13.
  **Time** 45 min.
1. The novices of the White Fields bleed the poppy from dawn to dusk in cloth masks that don't work; half are sleepers;
   the overseer, **Brother Pyke**, "the Gardener", buries the ones who die on the mounds, "to feed the flowers". Ide's
   friend died yesterday. She's next for the Abbey's "vigil".
2. The fields: the drying sheds; the novices' hut (straw, no blankets); the fresh graves; Pyke among his flowers, singing,
   an old man with dirt to the elbows who loves the poppy like children.
3. **The turn**: the Gardener's ledger shows the trade: the Abbey's milk to Gullhaven, the Regency's tenth, Carrow's
   barrels, the Greenhood's "losses" from Lantern carts (bought, not stolen: Wren pays the Gardener, it's cheaper than
   raiding). Every faction's seal in one book (`white_ledger`: evidence for MQ21's poppy layer and MQ22).
4. Choice (`white_fields`):
   - **Burn the fields** (`burned`): a fire across the hills, the novices freed, Pyke dead in his flowers or saved; the
     trade dies for a season; the Lantern (-30), the Regency (-10), Nell and the Greenhood (-10) all lose; the Ditch's and
     the Milk House's sleepers go into withdrawal at once (some die: a slide); Odalys, told why, does nothing to stop it.
   - **Free the novices only** (`freed`): Ide and twelve others to Gorse Hollow or the Hollow Oak; Lantern -15.
   - **Take the ledger and leave** (`ledger_only`).
   - **Report to Odalys** (`to_odalys`): she comes, sees, sends Pyke to the Abbey in chains; the fields remain ("the Order
     needs the coin"); `odalys_doubts`.
- **Rewards**: 260 xp.

## Contracts

### CT-BR1 — The Barrow-Hounds
- **Board** (Elderwick): *"Dead dogs. Dead, walking dogs. 160 silver. — M. Cotes."* **Level** 13. **Time** 20 min.
1. Five **barrow-hounds** (skeletal wolves, **new**) and their **Kennel-Master**, a draugr huntsman with a horn.
2. **The turn**: the huntsman's barrow was opened by Haskel's robbers; the hounds hunt the living because their master
   hunts for his stolen horn (on a robber's belt in Elderwick's inn). Return it and blow it at dawn: they go home
   (`rested`); or destroy them all.
- **Rewards**: 220 xp; 160 silver; the **huntsman's horn** (if destroyed).

### CT-BR2 — The Wight of Coldstep
- **Board** (Kingsmere, posted by Carrow's envoy): *"A dead thing holds Coldstep Pass. 300 silver from the Duchy of
  Carrow."* **Level** 15. **Time** 25 min.
1. **Coldstep Pass**: a **barrow-wight** (level 16) on a cairn, frozen guards round it.
2. **The turn**: it guards the old border the Oath set: no barrow-iron crosses the mountains. And on the far side,
   in the snow, the hero can see Carrow's army camp: tents, banners, thousands, waiting.
3. Kill it (`opened`: Carrow's couriers and soldiers can cross: they're in the siege as a third side, attacking the
   *living* from the east unless `sabeline_defected` or `carrow_pact`), or leave it and lie to Sabeline (`left`).
- **Rewards**: 300 xp; 300 silver (if killed).
