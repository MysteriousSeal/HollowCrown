# Hollowmere — Kingsmere, the lake, Reedby, Elderwick (and the Middle Downs)

**Tiles** x 1450–2750, z 1400–2700 (the Middle Downs 1450–2600, 2700–3800). **Levels** 4–8 (the isle 9–11). **Time**
~6 h of side content (10 side quests, 2 contracts). **Mood**: a wide grey-blue lake, reeds, herons, a walled town of
red roofs, a gallows always busy; outside the south wall, the Ditch.

Main quests here: MQ05, MQ06, MQ12, MQ16, MQ20, MQ21, MQ24, MQ25.

## Side quests

### SQ-HM1 — The Weed Rats
- **Giver**: Tom Weir. **Level** 6. **Time** 30 min.
1. Lake raiders take a "toll" from every Reedby boat; they took Hester Cole's last coin and her boat.
2. Their camp in the reeds (by boat, or wading at night): two raiders are Tom's own sons, **Dab** and **Finch**, gone over
   because Reedby starves under the fish tax. Lanky Moll keeps them in line with milk.
3. Choice (`hm_weed_rats`): **clear the camp** (the sons fight unless the hero stops at Moll; spared, sent home:
   Reedby +2) (`cleared`); **deal**: Moll leaves Reedby alone for the tax clerk's route (`dealt`: Regency -10, Reedby +1;
   next quarter a clerk is found in the reeds with his throat cut); **recruit them for the Greenhood** (Greenhood ≥ 20)
   (`to_greenhood`).
- **Rewards**: 200 xp; 60 silver or Moll's boat.

### SQ-HM2 — The Sunken Hall
- **Giver**: a book in the Lantern Chapel, or Agnes Lark. **Level** 7. **Time** 35 min.
1. Osric's first hall sank when the lake rose 300 years ago. Low water this summer: the roof breaks the surface.
2. Three flooded rooms (breath), drowned skeletons, an air pocket in the treasury.
3. Osric's **marriage stone**: the Oath carved as a marriage pact; and, scratched under it in a different hand, in
   barrow-tongue, a line the hero can't read until SQ-BR5 or Grey Edda reads it: *"He took me on the night my mother
   burned. This stone is a liar. — S."* (`osric_stone`)
- **Rewards**: 220 xp; **Osric's ring** (+1 all attributes); 150 silver of old coin.

### SQ-HM3 — Aldous's Lesson
- **Giver**: Aldous, in secret, after MQ05. **Level** 6. **Time** 40 min.
1. Aldous wants to see "the real Vale" without guards, one night, there and back by dawn.
2. Three stops, each a question (`aldous_just` or `aldous_hard` by the hero's answers):
   - **The Ditch**: a Barrowborn child dead of milk on a grave, a hood's vial in her hand. Aldous vomits. *"Who sold it
     to her?"* (The Greenhood; the Lantern grew it; his father taxed it: the hero can say any of it.)
   - **Reedby**: Hester Cole's family; Aldous gives away his cloak and his rings.
   - **The gallows at dawn**: a poacher about to hang. Aldous can be told to order him freed (he does, shaking) or to let
     the law be.
3. Corvin finds out (he always does): furious, or (Regency ≥ 40) quietly grateful: *"He needed to see it. I couldn't
   show him."*
- **Rewards**: 180 xp; the **heir's signet**; Regency ±5.

### SQ-HM4 — The Gallows Choir
- **Giver**: **Molly Fletch**, at the gallows. **Level** 7. **Time** 35 min. **After** MQ06.
1. Her brother **Tam** hangs at dawn for poaching a royal swan (or **Pip Tanner**, if `mq10_raid: warned`; or **Ash
   Rowan**, if `oak_wedding: taken`).
2. Ways: **the law** (the old charter: royal swans are the *king's*; there is no king: argue it before the magistrate,
   needs Regency ≥ 0 or the heir's signet) (`law`); **the rope** (cut him down at dawn with the Greenhood; a riot;
   Greenhood +15, Regency -20) (`rescue`); **the purse** (100 silver to Orme) (`bribe`); **let it be** (`hanged`: Molly
   sings at the gallows every night after; Kingsmere -1).
- **Rewards**: 200 xp.

### SQ-HM5 — The Steward's Pen
- **Giver**: Corvin (Regency ≥ 20) or Hiram Bose (`mq06_cart: split`). **Level** 8. **Time** 40 min.
1. Someone in the Hall sells Carrow the garrison numbers and the poppy routes.
2. Clues: a copied page sold at the Heron and Pike; a Carrow-blue thread on a seal; Orme's bribe (SQ-HM4); a girl at the
   Velvet Lamp, **Pell's niece Rosie** (she left Brindleford in the Wet Years), who says the steward pays her in Carrow
   coin and talks in his sleep.
3. **Orme**: he sold secrets to pay the Hall guards' wages, because the poppy purse had been skimmed, by someone
   higher: Captain Brayne. Orme has proof.
4. Choice (`orme`): **expose Orme** (`exposed`: hanged; Brayne untouched; Regency +15; Sabeline's eyes in the Hall
   blinded); **expose Brayne with Orme's proof** (`brayne_exposed`: Corvin hangs Brayne and flogs Orme; the Hall guard
   in MQ25 has a better captain, and no pogrom order comes from it); **turn Orme** (`turned`: Sabeline fed lies; her
   pact counts as 1 ally); **warn him** (`warned`: he leaves the water-gate unbarred in MQ20).
- **Rewards**: 220 xp; 120 silver or Orme's keys.

### SQ-HM6 — Lights in the Reeds
- **Giver**: Reeve Margery Cotes (Elderwick). **Level** 8. **Time** 30 min.
1. Lights over the lake lure Elderwick's boats onto the rocks; two fishers drowned.
2. On the water at night: the lights are **Lady Rhosyn** and **Lord Osbert Hale**'s drowned companions (and Rhosyn
   herself, a woman in sodden grey silk, searching, calling *"Maelis"*).
3. **Rite of Rest** (`rested`), or the fight (4 ghosts, Rhosyn among them; level 8) (`destroyed`). Laid to rest, Rhosyn
   tells the hero: *"The ferryman came back for her. Tell him I saw. Tell him I'm not angry. Tell him I'm so tired of
   being angry."* (`saw_maelis_saved`; `rhosyn_message` for Garrick: if delivered, he weeps and `garrick_trusts`).
- **Rewards**: 180 xp; Elderwick +1.

### SQ-HM7 — Elderwick's Wall
- **Giver**: Margery Cotes. **Level** 8. **Time** 35 min. **After** MQ12.
1. Elderwick is the closest village to the barrows; Margery wants the palisade done.
2. Timber (fell trees or hire an Oakhallow crew), iron for the gate (Gunnhild 40 silver, or Tobin free), a bell from the
   bell-founder (a cart, a draugr ambush on the north shore).
3. **The turn**: the village wants the wall to run *inside* the Barrowborn lane, leaving it outside ("the dead won't
   touch their own"). The hero can argue it out (Kind/Blunt; `wall_whole`), or let it be (`wall_lane_out`: in MQ22 the
   lane is overrun first).
- **Rewards**: 200 xp; Elderwick +2 (`elderwick_wall`).

### SQ-HM8 — The Nurse's Garden
- **Giver**: Old Nurse Bettony, talking to the roses. **Level** 6. **Time** 25 min.
1. She believes Maelis lives ("her rosebush still flowers"). She wants the princess's music box from the shut-up royal
   apartments.
2. The apartments (sneak in, or ask Aldous): dust; a child's room; the music box; and **Edric's unsent letter** to his
   daughter: *"Tomorrow I sign away the barrows, and you to Haakon, and I'm sorry for the second more than the first.
   Corvin says the dead will wake. I say we'll starve. Corvin also says Haakon's wives died of falls. One of us is
   right about both."* (`edric_letter`)
3. Bettony plays it and weeps. In Act III, she recognises Maelis on sight (a public witness).
- **Rewards**: 150 xp; Kingsmere +1.

### SQ-HM9 — The Envoy's Table
- **Giver**: Sabeline's invitation, delivered by a Carrow page after MQ15 (if not `sold`). **Level** 10. **Time** 40 min.
1. Dinner at the Envoy's House: Carrow food (eel in honey, blood sausage, white wine), a lake terrace, candles; a duel of
   questions: each round the hero answers one of hers and asks one (tones). She lies about her missing finger ("a
   duel"); the hero can catch the lie if they've seen oath-iron frost on a sworn hand.
2. **The turn**: halfway through, a Carrow courier arrives; Sabeline reads the letter and her face does nothing at all:
   Ferrand's reminder that Haakon is "eager for the match". The hero can ask (Kind) or read it off the table (Sly).
3. Romance: the scene, if affection ≥ 3 ([romances.md](../romances.md)). Otherwise she sends the hero home at midnight
   with a bottle and the truth about one thing: Ketter's daughter is held at Ferrand's court (for `lina_freed`).
- **Rewards**: 200 xp; Sabeline's **steel fan** (a parrying off-hand).

### SQ-HM10 — Songs from the Ditch
- **Giver**: Old Grey Edda, singing at the gallows (MQ05). **Level** 8. **Time** 40 min.
1. Edda wants three things before she dies: her grandson **Fen**, a sleeper, off the graves of the old cemetery; the
   Greenhood's sellers out of the Ditch; and someone *not sworn* to hear the true songs.
2. **Fen**: on his grandmother's grave, poppy-eyed, talking to her. Getting him off it: carry him (he fights), or sit with
   him and listen to who he's talking to (Kind; it's his mother, who died in the Wet Years in the reeves' seed seizure).
3. **The sellers**: two hoods selling milk to children. Talk them out (Greenhood ≥ 20: Wren's name), drive them out
   (Greenhood -10), or report them (the herons come and hang them; and beat half the Ditch while they're at it).
4. **The songs**: at night, Edda sings Cairnfold: the doors, the lanterns, Sigrun. The hero hears the Vale's history from
   the other side (`ditch_songs`: lore; with `osric_stone` she reads Sigrun's line; title *Grey-friend*).
5. Choice: promise Edda the Barrowborn laws will end "when there's a crown" (`edda_promise`), or don't lie to her.
- **Rewards**: 220 xp; the Barrowborn as an ally (MQ24).

## Contracts

### CT-HM1 — The Toll-Keeper
- **Board** (Kingsmere): *"Travellers robbed and killed at the Old Toll Bridge. 120 silver."* **Level** 6. **Time** 20 min.
1. Bones in the stream; coins on the parapet, untouched.
2. **The Toll-Keeper**: a draugr in rusted royal livery, rising at dusk for "the king's penny"; he hanged, in life,
   anyone who couldn't pay, on Osric's order.
3. Pay him a royal penny (the Sunken Hall's coin) (`paid`: title *Bridgekeeper*) or fight (2 skeletons).
- **Rewards**: 160 xp; 120 silver.

### CT-HM2 — The Weeping Willows
- **Board** (Reedby): *"Something sings in the Willows and folk walk into the lake. 80 silver. — T. Weir."* **Level** 7.
  **Time** 20 min.
1. A ghost, **Lissa**, singing the drowned to her: she drowned herself after her betrothed was hanged for poaching.
2. **The turn**: her betrothed wasn't hanged for poaching; he was hanged for killing the reeve who raped her (implied in
   the court roll's "for assault upon the person of the reeve, and his death"). His grave is outside the wall. Bring his
   ring to the willows and she goes quiet (`rested`), or fight her and two drowned.
- **Rewards**: 140 xp; 80 silver; Reedby +1.
