# Brindle Vale — the starting region

**Tiles** x 350–1450, z 2900–3900. **Levels** 1–4. **Time** ~6.5 h (prologue 2.5 h, side quests 3.5 h, contract
0.5 h). **Mood**: a gentle green valley at the end of summer: birch woods, hay meadows, a slow river, a chapel on the
hill, bees, the smell of bread. Under it: a famine pit nobody mentions, a poppy patch on it, and the first cold of the
failing Oath.

Main quests here: MQ01–MQ04 ([main-quest-1.md](../main-quest-1.md)).

A stone post with a lantern niche, an offering bowl, a bench. West, the pass is closed by a rockslide (the map's edge).
On the road east, the murdered old pilgrim woman (MQ01): Hesper Rowe's mother.

### Chapel of the Quiet Bell (1080, 3180), the famine pit (1090, 3200), the Bellwarden's Tomb
- **The chapel**: roofless, half a bell tower, the great green bronze bell, an altar, graves on the slope.
- **The famine pit**: a long low mound against the chapel wall; sixty of the Wet Years' dead; grave-poppy thick on it,
  stems cut (Jory). After MQ01 the turf is broken from inside.
- **The crypt** (MQ02): the Ossuary, the Bell Hall, the Warden's Rest (details in MQ02). After: quiet, or 3 skeletons
  every 3 days if Hamund was destroyed.

### Red Hen camp (620, 3560)
A birch clearing: 5x5 palisade, two tents, a fire, a weapon rack, crates of stolen pilgrim goods and poppy vials, the
gate facing east, torches. The stock (Wat). The back tent (Hesper, chained). 5 bandits (level 2–3) and Brannoc
(level 4). After MQ03: empty, a burned tent, Hesper's chain on the ground.

### Mossjaw Cave (1300, 3650)
A mouth in a gorse knoll. Two chambers and a deep tunnel: wolves at the mouth; a **pit-eater**'s nest in the first chamber;
spiders, the brood mother; a side crack (Wenna) (SQ-BV6).

### Hob's Tower (1400, 3000)
A ruined round watchtower over the Pilgrim Road; the Regency checkpoint at its foot (Sergeant Matthias Crow, 3
soldiers); at night, howling (CT-BV1).

## Side quests

### SQ-BV1 — The Flour Thief
- **Giver**: Dunstan (or the board: *"Flour stolen. Reward. — Dunstan"*). **Level** 2. **Time** 25 min. **After** MQ01.
1. Sacks vanish from the loft at night. Dunstan blames "that orphan".
2. A night in the loft: **Kit** creeps in and sweeps spilled flour into a rag; he never touches the sacks. Later **Jory**
   loads two sacks on a hand-cart, sweating in the cold, lips blue.
3. Follow Jory: first to the **famine pit**, where he cuts poppy heads into a jar, kneeling on the graves of people he
   knew, talking to someone (*"I know, Mam. Soon. I know."*); then to the **Hanging Oak**, where he trades the flour for
   vials of milk.
4. Confronting him: he's been a sleeper since the Wet Years, when his mother died in the pit. The poppy lets him talk to
   her. The Red Hen's men want more each week.
5. Choice (`bv_flour`):
   - **Tell Dunstan** (`jory_told`): Dunstan beats his son bloody in the yard and locks him in the loft to sweat it out;
     Jory survives withdrawal or doesn't (he does if the hero brings Nan Wicket's draught: a short task).
   - **Make him stop and pay it back quietly** (`jory_quiet`): he promises; he doesn't stop; the hero finds him again in
     Act II, worse, at the pit (an encounter: he's started to dig).
   - **Report him to Pell** (`jory_reeve`): a week in the stocks; the village jeers; he hangs himself in the mill in Act
     II (a slide, and a line from Dunstan if the hero comes back).
   - **Bring him to Cuthwin and Nan Wicket** to be weaned (Kind; the hero must pay Nan 10 copper and visit twice)
     (`jory_weaned`): Brindleford +1; Jory clean by Act II; he's the one who first tells the hero the pit dead "aren't
     angry, they're just hungry".
   - Any time: **speak for Kit** (`kit_at_mill`): Dunstan takes him on.
- **Rewards**: 80 xp; 25 copper; a flour sack (5 bread).

### SQ-BV2 — Kit's Father
- **Giver**: Kit, after SQ-BV1 or the third talk. **Level** 2–9 (stages). **Time** 30 min in all.
1. Kit's father, **Pip Tanner**, went to Kingsmere four years ago to earn money and never came back. Kit has his
   whittled wooden heron.
2. **Kingsmere** (after MQ04): the levy roll: pressed into the Regency, deserted three years ago, "to hang if found".
3. **Pip** is Wren's second (MQ06 or the Hollow Oak). The heron: he sits down hard. He couldn't go home a deserter.
4. Choice (`kit`): **bring Kit to the Hollow Oak** (`kit_with_pip`); **bring Pip home** (needs Greenhood ≥ 20 or a
   pardon from SQ-HM4) (`pip_home`: Brindleford +1, Greenhood -5); **tell Kit his father is dead** "for his good"
   (`kit_lied`); or bring the news if Pip was hanged (`pip_hanged`).
- **Rewards**: 150 xp; the wooden heron (a trinket, +1 luck).

### SQ-BV3 — Honey and Wax
- **Giver**: Agna Bee. **Level** 3. **Time** 30 min. **After** MQ02.
1. Agna's bees desert their hives and die in the meadow. Little Brede: *"They go to the cold stones and fall asleep."*
2. **The Nine Sisters**: frost on the stones in daylight; the mound dug into; an **oath-iron grave-ward** thrown in the
   grass; boot prints and candle-wax.
3. Night at the stones: 3 ghosts and the **Barrow-Maiden** (level 4): a hill-girl buried 400 years ago with a bronze
   necklace; she only wants it back (it's in the digger's hands).
4. Set the ward back (only the hero can carry it).
5. The wax leads to **Hal Wicke**: he dug for barrow gold to pay Garrick's bride-price for Elsa; he has the necklace.
   Choice (`hal_secret`): **tell Agna and Goody Thatch** (`told`: Hal fined, shamed; Tallow Green +1) or **keep it**
   (`kept`: he gives the necklace back himself, at the stones at night, terrified; owes the hero).
- **Rewards**: 120 xp; honeycomb ×5; Tallow Green's honey stocked again.

### SQ-BV4 — A Bell With No Tongue
- **Giver**: Cuthwin, given the Bell-Tongue. **Level** 3. **Time** 25 min.
1. He wants the bell rung by the living, not the dead.
2. A new yoke-pin from **Tobin** (2 iron bars, or 30 copper). The half-ruined tower to climb (ledges, a ladder to mend).
3. Hang the tongue; ring it at dawn: all Brindleford comes up the hill. Old Meg leaves flowers on the famine pit.
4. Cuthwin, afterwards, on the chapel step: why he left the Order. *"The Lector chose a girl of fourteen for a vigil. He
   asked me to hold her head while Brother Hode sewed her eyes. I held it. For one stitch. Then I let go and ran, and
   I've been running for twelve years, and I never told anyone, and I never went back for her."* (`cuthwin_story`)
5. He teaches the **Rite of Rest** (`rite_of_rest`).
- **Rewards**: 120 xp; Brindleford +1 (`bv_bell_rung`).

### SQ-BV5 — The Reeve's Ledger
- **Giver**: Pell, nervous, after MQ03. **Level** 3. **Time** 30 min.
1. A Regency auditor, **Clerk Ansel Pike**, is coming. Pell wants him "delayed a day".
2. Pell keeps **two ledgers**; the true one shows a third of the tax kept back every year so the village eats. And a
   third ledger, older, from the Wet Years: the seed grain Pell seized for Kingsmere, house by house, and against six
   names a later mark: *pit*.
3. Pike arrives: thin, polite, a collector of hanged men's fingers in a box (he shows it as a joke; it isn't one).
4. Choice (`pell_ledger`): **delay Pike and say nothing** (`hidden`: Brindleford +1, Regency -5); **give Pike the true
   ledger** (`reported`: Pell taken to Kingsmere and hanged; **Reeve Grimwald** comes; Brindleford -2, Regency +15);
   **show Pike the risen dead** and get the missing tax written off as war losses (needs MQ02 done) (`written_off`);
   or **make Pell tell the village about the pit ledger** (Blunt; `pell_confessed`): the village sits in silence in the
   inn; Old Meg slaps him; then the village decides to give the pit dead a proper burial with Cuthwin (if SQ-BV4 done:
   the famine ghosts never return; Brindleford +1 in the long run).
- **Rewards**: 100 xp; 40 or 60 copper.

### SQ-BV6 — Mossjaw
- **Giver**: Ada Cobbe, frantic (or Scrap the dog, barking). **Level** 4. **Time** 40 min. **After** MQ01.
1. Wenna went after a lost ewe two days ago. Hob Cobbe says she'll turn up; he won't look at anyone.
2. Scrap leads across the Shepherds' track to **Mossjaw**: 3 wolves at the mouth; in the first chamber the ewe's remains, and
   the one who ate it: **old Aldo**, a pit-eater (level 4), a Brindleford man who vanished in the Wet Years; he flees the
   hero's torch, and he left Wenna alone because she gave him her bread (if spared, he's seen once more, leaving a
   rabbit at the Cobbes' door); spiders in the second chamber; the **brood mother** in the deep tunnel (level 4); behind her, in a crack too narrow for her,
   Wenna, starving, bruised, alive, with a knife.
3. **The turn**: she didn't follow a ewe. She ran. Hob owes the Red Hen for poppy and has sold a year of her labour to
   Brannoc's camp to pay it: hauling water, gutting game, mending stolen clothes. He told Ada she'd be "in service in
   Tallow Green". He beats her when he's sick for the white. She'd rather the spiders.
4. Choice (`wenna`):
   - **Take her home** (`home`): Ada believes Hob, or chooses to; Wenna runs again in a week. (If the Red Hen camp still
     stands, she's taken there.)
   - **Tell Ada** with the bruises as proof (Blunt or Kind) (`ada_told`): Ada throws Hob out; he drinks in the inn and
     hates the hero; Wenna stays with Ada.
   - **Confront Hob** (Hard: a beating; `hob_beaten`): he swears off; he doesn't; Ada leaves him in Act II.
   - **Take her to Nan Wicket** to live and learn herbs (`wenna_nan`): she becomes Nan's apprentice; in the epilogue,
     Brindleford's herbalist.
   - With MQ03 done: Hob's debt died with the Red Hen; with Brannoc `to_wren` or `spared`, the debt still stands.
- **Rewards**: 160 xp; the Cobbes' **wool cloak** (if Ada); Wenna's **sling**; Brindleford +1.

### SQ-BV7 — The Smith's Apprentice
- **Giver**: Tobin. **Level** 3. **Time** 20 min (completes at once if Wat was freed in MQ03).
1. Wat ran off with Tobin's best blade "to be a bandit".
2. In the Red Hen camp's stock: he refused to cut the old pilgrim woman's throat; another did; they've been teaching
   him. He saw what was done in the back tent and won't say it.
3. Home: Tobin says *"Good"* and holds him a long time. Then the **smith-book**: his grandfather's drawings of a crown
   and a blue fire. *"My people kept the Oathforge, before the Lantern took it. I could find my way round an old forge."*
   (`tobin_at_forge` possible.)
- **Rewards**: 100 xp; **Harrow steel** (keep it) or a free upgrade later (return it).

### SQ-BV8 — A Candle for Elsa
- **Giver**: Elsa, crying in the yard; or Hal. **Level** 4. **Time** 30 min. **After** MQ03.
1. Elsa and Hal want to marry; Garrick forbids it and demands a bride-price no chandler's son could pay.
2. Hal (honest or sullen by `hal_secret`).
3. Garrick, at the river: the hero can push with what they know:
   - `bv_bell_rung`: *"You gave this village its nerve back. Give your daughter hers."*
   - MQ11 or MQ12 known: *"You're afraid a son-in-law will ask where the money came from. And who Rhosyn was."* (he goes
     white; Elsa, behind the hero, hears the name and asks; Garrick tells her: *"Someone I didn't save."*)
   - Plainly, Elsa's happiness (weakest).
4. Success: the **wedding** at the Ferryman's Rest (music, dancing in the square, Kit steals cake, Tobin's speech:
   *"Good."*; Garrick dances once, badly) (`bv_elsa_wed`; `garrick_trusts`). Failure: Elsa and Hal elope to Kingsmere
   (`bv_elsa_eloped`; Garrick drinks; Brindleford -1).
- **Rewards**: 120 xp; Brindleford +1; a wedding candle (never burns down).

### CT-BV1 — The Watch at Hob's Tower
- **Board** (after MQ02): *"Something howls in Hob's Tower at night. My lads won't stand watch. 60 copper. — Sgt. M.
  Crow."* **Level** 4. **Time** 25 min.
1. Crow: three nights of howling; a soldier came down white-haired.
2. Night in the tower: two **skeleton hounds** (**new**, level 3) and **Corporal Hob Ashdown** (ghost, level 5), a
   watchman of the Barrow War, howling the alarm for an enemy 300 years gone.
3. Pell's archive: the garrison roll, and the last order, never delivered: *"The war is done. Stand down the watch."*
   And under it, in another hand: *"Burn what's left at Cairnfold."* (lore)
4. Choice (`hob_watch`): **give him the order** (`stood_down`: he salutes and fades; Lantern +5) or **destroy him**
   (`destroyed`: his **watchman's horn**).
- **Rewards**: 120 xp; 60 copper; the checkpoint waves the hero through.
